Miniature DC/DC

2025
Passed all the tests
KiCAD LTspice STM8 JLCPCB

The task was to make a miniature controlled converter with an output voltage indication. The converter is controlled by the STM8, switching is carried out by changing the resistance of the DAC, which in turn changes the feedback on the DCDC. At the same time, the STM8 measures the input and output voltages using the built-in ADC, displays the information on the screen, and polls the button.

  • Compact overall dimensions
  • Microprocessor control of the device operation
  • Embedded software developed to customer requirements

Panelization of USB-C

2025
Successfully manufactured
JLCPCB V-CUT Optimization Panelization KiCAD

A custom PCB design featuring carefully engineered panelization for six pairs USB-C connectors (both Female and Male). The board incorporates a distinctive V-CUT design, which improves segmentation and assembly. It also leverages advanced fresering manufacturing techniques for robust and efficient production. Additionally, I developed specialized soldering windows for the connectors, ensuring precise soldering during the factory assembly process.

  • Using V-CUT(V-scoring/V-grooving) technology when designing a PCB panel
  • Production optimization at the design stage
  • Increases the number of products on the panel

In Development

Redesigning a inverter board from an existing schematic into a modern, production-ready PCB. The project involves replacing obsolete components, updating the circuit with modern electronic elements, validating key sections in LTspice, and developing the PCB layout in KiCAD with a focus on high-current paths, thermal management, isolation, and reliable power delivery.
