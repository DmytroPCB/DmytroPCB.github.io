About Me

I started in electronics by learning the fundamentals of circuit design, components, and embedded systems. Over time, I taught myself PCB design because I wanted to turn ideas and schematics into real, working hardware. Since then, I've designed and built electronic circuits, PCBs, and hardware projects — learning, testing, and improving with every project.
